On calibration and precision--recall within dataset (NASA, averaged over the three tree ensembles and the four horizons): raw scores reach a mean PR-AUC of 0.774 while isotonic calibration collapses it to 0.639, because the fitted step function creates large tied groups; Platt preserves ranking.
