\item \texttt{cover\_letter\_ITEES.pdf} --- addressed to the Editor-in-Chief; leads with the ITEES continuity (extends Shikdar \& Laaksonen, ITEES 2026, DOI 10.1155/etep/6000810 --- ref [10]) and the ``controlled diagnosis, not a new model'' framing; closes with the APC-waiver request.

\section*{4. Upload sequence on the portal}
\begin{itemize}
\boxitem Article type: \textbf{Research Article}.
\boxitem Upload \texttt{main\_itees.pdf} as the main manuscript (portal accepts PDF) and the LaTeX source zip if requested.
\boxitem Upload the supplementary file separately (S1).
\boxitem Paste or attach \texttt{cover\_letter\_ITEES.pdf}.
\boxitem Suggested reviewers if asked: pick 2--3 from the reference-list authors with no conflict (different institutions, no co-authorship in the last 3 years).
\boxitem License: \textbf{CC BY 4.0}. APC: select the waiver/discount option --- Group~A countries are applied automatically; if the portal does not detect it, request the waiver in the funding step and cite the Research4Life Group~A list.
\boxitem Funding step: ``no specific grant'' (matches the manuscript's funding statement).
\end{itemize}

\section*{5. What to expect}
Editorial screening (scope, integrity, basic quality) first; papers outside scope or below the quality bar are declined here without review. Then peer review. Count on a few weeks for screening and roughly 3--4 months end-to-end if it goes to review; respond to any revision request within the stated window. If declined at screening, the next free-for-you option on the ladder remains \textit{Energy Science \& Engineering} (Wiley, Q2, same waiver), so the package can be re-targeted quickly.
