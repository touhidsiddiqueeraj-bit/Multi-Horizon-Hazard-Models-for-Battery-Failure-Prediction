Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript "Multi-Horizon Failure-Risk Models for Battery
Failure Prediction: How Much Cross-Chemistry Transfer Is a State-of-Health
Shortcut?" for consideration as a Full Length Research Article in the
Journal of Energy Storage.

Instead of remaining-useful-life regression, we study multi-horizon
failure-risk classification — whether a cell fails within the next H
cycles — and subject it to a validity analysis across eight datasets
(NASA, CALCE, Oxford, Severson, and 60 new Battery Archive NMC/NCA/LFP
cells), four model families, leakage-clean calibration, and an ESP32-S3
deployment. The central finding is a cautionary negative result: apparent
cross-dataset transfer is largely a State-of-Health shortcut, reproducible
by an unfitted threshold rule, while sensor features often invert under
shift. Same-chemistry cross-laboratory controls show dataset shift matters
at least as much as chemistry shift.
