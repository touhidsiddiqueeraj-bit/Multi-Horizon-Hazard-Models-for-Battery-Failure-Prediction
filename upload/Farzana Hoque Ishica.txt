Farzana Hoque Eshica is an undergraduate student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at Leading University, Bangladesh, pursuing a B.Sc. degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering. Alongside her studies in EEE, she has a strong interest in mathematics, physics, and computational methods. Her research interests include quantum physics, computational physics, electromagnetism, quantum technology, and data-driven modeling of physical and engineering systems. She is particularly interested in exploring interdisciplinary problems at the intersection of electrical engineering, physics, mathematics, and computation.
