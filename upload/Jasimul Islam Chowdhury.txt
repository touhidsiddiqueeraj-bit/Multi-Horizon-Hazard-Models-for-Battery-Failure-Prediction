Jasimul Islam Chowdhury received the B.Sc. degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Leading University, Sylhet, Bangladesh, in 2026. His research interests include power electronics, embedded systems, industrial automation, intelligent control systems, and AI-integrated engineering solutions. His final year thesis, "Design and Comparative Analysis of a PSO-PID Based LSTM Controlled DC-DC Buck-Boost Converter for Improved Transient Performance," explored hybrid control strategies combining particle swarm optimization, PID control, and deep learning for enhanced converter performance. He has authored work presented at IEEE conferences and is an active contributor to technical research communities, with additional project experience in AI-powered voice assistants and robotic and motor control systems. He is currently engaged in independent academic research.
